Turning practical experience into structured trainingMayar da Kwarewa Zuwa Horo Mai Tsari

Kamalu KNICE is a digital skills instructor and content creator passionate about helping individuals and businesses learn Artificial Intelligence, technology, content creation, graphic design, video editing, website development, and modern digital solutions.

Kamalu KNICE malamin fasahar zamani ne kuma mai kirkirar content, wanda ke da sha'awar taimaka wa mutane da kamfanoni su koyi Artificial Intelligence (AI), fasaha, kirkirar content, zanen hoto, gyaran bidiyo, gina shafukan yanar gizo, da sauran hanyoyin fasahar zamani.

What started as sharing tech tips on Facebook and YouTube grew into KNICE Digital Academy — a training academy built for people who learn best by doing, in the language they're most comfortable with.

Abin da ya fara a matsayin raba shawarwarin fasaha a Facebook da YouTube ya girma ya zama KNICE Digital Academy — makarantar horo da aka gina wa mutanen da suke koyo mafi kyau ta hanyar aiki, cikin harshen da suka fi jin dadi da shi.

MissionManufa

Why KNICE Digital Academy existsDalilin Kafa KNICE Digital Academy

To empower people with digital skills, Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools, content creation knowledge, and modern technology skills — regardless of where they're starting from.

Manufar makarantar ita ce ta ba mutane ilimin fasahar zamani, kayan aikin AI, ilimin kirkirar content, da fasahar zamani — ko da inda suke farawa daga.

  • Lessons taught in plain, practical language — English or Hausa.
  • Ana koyarwa cikin sauki da harshen da za a fahimta — Turanci ko Hausa.
  • Every course built around real tools and real projects, not just theory.
  • Kowane darasi an gina shi bisa ainihin kayan aiki da ayyuka, ba ka'idoji kawai ba.
  • Support that continues after class — via WhatsApp, Telegram and social media.
  • Ana ci gaba da taimako bayan darasi — ta WhatsApp, Telegram da shafukan sada zumunta.
